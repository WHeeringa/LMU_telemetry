Recording laps in LMU

Turn on Settings › Gameplay › Record Telemetry, or bind Telemetry Recording under Controls to start it per stint. Drive at least one full lap. LMU writes one file per session to:

…\steamapps\common\Le Mans Ultimate\UserData\Telemetry\

A file only holds your own car. To compare with a friend, they press Save lap A as file on the Laps page and send you the .json; open it here like any other file. Nothing is saved by this page: close or reload it and the laps are gone. Lap files (.json) from the earlier Lap Compare and Lap Coach pages open here too.

How grip loss is spotted

  • Lockup: a wheel turns more than 10% slower than the car while braking.
  • Wheelspin: a rear wheel turns more than 8% faster than the car on throttle.
  • Oversteer estimate: you steer against the direction of the corner (opposite lock).
  • Understeer estimate: much more steering than the car's path needs, compared with your own normal ratio on that lap.
  • Off track: two or more wheels on grass or gravel.
  • ABS and TC interventions are shaded grey on the charts.

About the numbers

Laps are lined up by LMU's own lap distance, so "200 m" is the same spot on track for every driver. The time delta is lap A minus lap B at each point: above zero means A is behind.

The file format was taken from published notes on LMU's recordings. If something looks wrong for your car or track, the raw file will tell us why.

Real track heights

Hills in the camera and the Height chart come from, in this order: a height channel in the recording (LMU doesn't write one today), a TinyPedal track map, or an estimate from how the car accelerates.

Built in: Le Mans, Spa, Monza (and Curva Grande), Imola, Interlagos, COTA, Fuji, Lusail, Portimão (Algarve) and the Sebring School circuit. For another track, put its TinyPedal track map .svg in the trackmaps folder next to this page (and add the file name to trackmaps/index.json), or drop the .svg on the page for this visit only. Maps in the folder win over the built-in ones.
